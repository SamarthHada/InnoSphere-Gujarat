Submission Flow

What happens after submission.

The record is stored as a pending project, then shown in the dashboard for approval and analytics.

  • 1. Capture basics Title and domain are collected first so users stay focused on the project identity.
  • 2. Add ownership and funding Researcher and budget context are entered in the next step.
  • 3. Attach the paper Drag and drop a PDF research paper or browse from the device.
Tip for users Keep the title specific, the description concise, and attach a PDF only when it adds real context for the reviewer.
